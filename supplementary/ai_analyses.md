# AI analyses — attribution and status

**Status: NON-PEER-REVIEWED. Tool-generated context. Not part of the
measured results. The raw transcripts are NOT archived in this
repository.**

## What exists

Two independent analyses were conducted outside the measured pipeline,
using large-language-model assistants:

1. **Z.ai (GLM) analysis** — conducted in the authoring sessions, reaching
   a qualitative conclusion that structural complexity of pawn-endgame
   value functions concentrates where material is balanced ("complexity
   concentrates at balance").
2. **Google Gemini analysis** — conducted independently, arriving at a
   similar qualitative balance-concentration claim from a different
   starting point.

Both are reported by the repository owner; the transcripts are not
committed here, so **the exact wording, prompts, and reasoning of those
analyses cannot be verified from this repository**. They are cited in
Paper 1 Sec. 7 only as (a) context for the balance reframe and (b) an
example of independent qualitative claims that the measured data are
consistent with. They are excluded from the author lists by design, and
they contribute **no numbers** to FACTS.md — every number in the
traceability table comes from an artifact produced by the measured
pipeline.

## Why they are mentioned at all

The balance reframe in Paper 1 (piece count -> material balance as the
operative variable) was made *after* the class measurements, from the
class pattern itself. The external analyses are recorded because they
predicted the pattern qualitatively, from different starting points,
before the class protocol existed; that is context, not evidence. If the
transcripts are ever recovered, they should be archived here with their
prompts; until then, this note is the complete provenance.
